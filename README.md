# THE GLOBAL FRONT PAGE — news-image-20261121 (v3)

The flagship maximal broadsheet for Saturday 21 November 2026: an illustrated lead on the Russian missiles that fell in Poland (night sky, missile trails over a border village, a crashed missile in a field), a dot-matrix world map with numbered pins keyed to story numbers, a UTC timeline rail, and 43+ numbered stories under colour-banded region headers — EUROPE, NORTH AMERICA, MIDDLE EAST, ASIA-PACIFIC, SOUTH ASIA, AFRICA and LATIN AMERICA, plus CLIMATE/COP31, HEALTH and SPORT bands. The date appears in the masthead strip, in the standfirst line, and once more in a black DATE CHECK strip near the bottom. The page (entry.png) is rendered by render.py (PIL, Liberation fonts in fonts/) and main.py copies the committed page to the output directory.
